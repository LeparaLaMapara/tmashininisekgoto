Leadership progression

Building yourself out of the critical path

STAGE 01
Solve
I solve the problem myself
STAGE 02
Pattern
We turn the solution into something repeatable
STAGE 03
Teach
The team can run the pattern without me
STAGE 04
Federate
The wider engineering organisation adopts and supports it
STAGE 05
Step back
It keeps working when I am not in the room
Different parts of a capability sit at different stages. Knowing which is which is most of the job.
